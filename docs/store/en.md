## Description
Animals that can't fly, flying anyway. Tilt your phone to steer a turtle in a shell jet, a penguin belly-gliding, a hamster in a ball copter, a cat on a magic broom, a frog with a balloon body or a bunny with ear copters — and thread the rings.

THREAD IT CLEAN
Every ring judges you: BULLSEYE, GREAT, OK or MISS, by how close to the centre you fly. Chain passes for a combo. Rings shrink while you hesitate, tilt into slits, and drift sideways.

TODAY'S COURSE
One course per day, generated from the date, identical for every player in the world. Beat your own ghost — your best run flies beside you.

SKY RUN
An endless course that tightens the further you get. Three misses and it's over. One more try.

FLY YOUR WAY
Three sensitivity levels from a gentle, auto-levelling Easy to an Expert mode with real airspeed and stalls. Boost is a gauge that stars refill.

COME BACK TOMORROW
Three daily quests, a day streak, character mastery levels, and trail colours you earn.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. No ads. No tracking. Game Center leaderboards, a daily reminder, iCloud sync and clip sharing are all opt-in and off until you turn them on.

Rated 4+. Landscape. Needs a gyroscope (iPhone; drag-to-steer on iPad without one).
